Research note · sequel to the referee report

A Road Through the ALE Fiber

Can the figure-eight/E₆ paper be connected to actual physics? This note records the one road I consider defensible, the dictionary it induces, two computations performed today that make the dictionary concrete — one of which verifies a claim the paper left uncited — and the named obstructions that keep this a proposal rather than a result. Nothing here claims a unification of anything.

1 · The construction

The McKay step of the paper — 2T ↦ E₆ — has a standard physical home: ℂ²/2T is the E₆ ALE singularity, and gauge symmetry of type E₆ lives on such a singularity in M-theory. The proposal is therefore:

Take the 7-dimensional internal geometry X = (ℂ²/2T-fiber) over Q, with Q = S³∖4₁ the figure-eight knot complement. M-theory on ℝ^{3,1} × X, near the singular locus, is 7-dimensional E₆ super-Yang–Mills on ℝ^{3,1} × Q, partially twisted along Q. In the twisted theory the three normal-bundle scalars combine with the gauge field into a complexified connection, and the BPS vacuum equations say: vacua = flat E₆(ℂ) connections on Q.

The paper already possesses the canonical such vacuum: ρ₀ = principal ∘ ρ_geo, the hyperbolic holonomy pushed through the principal SL₂ — the exact analogue of the “geometric connection” that dominates the 3d–3d correspondence for M5-branes on the same manifold. Note what this does to the paper’s ledger: the principal placement (C6), a priced stipulation there, becomes here a candidate physical datum — the twist homomorphism of the compactification.

4 · The obstructions, named

O-1No G₂ metric

A hyperbolic Q is negatively curved; the naive product or flat-twist of the ALE fiber over Q is not Ricci-flat, and no G₂-holonomy metric fibering ℂ²/2T over a hyperbolic 3-manifold is known. What survives without it is the partially twisted field theory (well-defined on any Q, which is how the BPS counting above makes sense) — a supergravity-exact background does not currently exist. This is genuine open research, not a computation.

O-2Chirality — and the paper’s own Niven theorem is the roadblock

Matter from flat-bundle cohomology on Q is vector-like: the twisted Euler characteristic vanishes, so h² = h¹ and every 27 zero-mode is paired. Genuinely chiral 4d matter in this class of compactifications comes from isolated points where the singularity enhances — for 27’s of E₆, points of local E₇ enhancement. And the paper proves (Cor. 7.5) that the E₇ end is arithmetically impossible for this manifold: ℚ(√2) would require the irrational meridian trace x=√2. In this frame the paper’s celebrated “E₇ exclusion” reads as: this object refuses the standard mechanism for chiral generations at its rational ends. Any road to a chiral spectrum must enter through different, unbuilt geometry.

O-3Gravity is ambient, not derived

In this frame 4d gravity is the ordinary supergravity zero-mode of the ambient theory. The knot would be the origin of the gauge/matter sector at best; nothing about it unifies or explains gravitation. “SM ⊗ GR from the figure-eight knot” is not on this road — “gauge sector engineered on a distinguished manifold, gravity as usual” is.

O-4The cusp, and a tension with the arithmetic

Q is noncompact (one cusp); a compact model needs a Dehn filling or an end condition, and the paper’s own §6 records that the surveyed fillings destroy exactly the arithmetic (the V₄ of fields) that produced 2T and E₆. The entrance and compactness pull in opposite directions; whether an end condition exists that keeps both is open.

5 · Sober summary

The road exists and is ordinary physics: geometric engineering of an E₆ gauge sector on the E₆ ALE singularity, with the figure-eight complement as the defect locus, its hyperbolic holonomy (through the principal twist) as the canonical vacuum, six neutral moduli, and three vector-like 27 multiplets — the last number verified today and structural. Three of the paper’s worst self-diagnosed pathologies become features in this frame (non-reality → complexified connection; rank ceiling → the 27-Higgs necessity, with the 27’s present; C6 → the twist datum). What blocks the road is equally sharp: no G₂ metric over a hyperbolic base (open geometry), no chirality — with the paper’s own E₇-exclusion theorem sitting exactly on the standard chirality mechanism — and gravity untouched. As unification, no. As a well-posed research question — does any G₂ background realize the E₆ ALE fibration over a hyperbolic homology-sphere-like base, and can its enhancement points evade the Niven obstruction? — it is real, currently unanswered, and the only version of “physics from this paper” I would defend.
