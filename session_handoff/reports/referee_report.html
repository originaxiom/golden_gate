Referee report · verified scrutiny

Golden Gate Referee Report

On: “From minimal description to E₆: a chain through the figure-eight knot complement” (D. Mehmetaj, dated 19 Aug 2026; main.tex, 3 343 lines, + verify/ suite, 19 scripts).
Method: every criticism below was verified before being written. The full shipped suite was executed (19/19 pass), and the central claims were re-derived independently — from the E₆ Cartan matrix, the quaternion groups, the knot-group relator, and the number field, without reusing the paper’s code, except where explicitly noted.

Verdict: the mathematics that can be checked is essentially all correct — I found no false theorem — but the paper has one genuine unproved proposition (Prop. 7.7, “2880”), one internal contradiction, an unshipped third of its own verification table, and a serious genre problem: roughly half the text is meta-commentary about its own drafting history rather than mathematics.

Recommendation: major revision. The theorems survive hostile checking remarkably well; the manuscript as a document does not.

1 · What was verified independently and stands

Each item below was recomputed from scratch in exact arithmetic (Fraction/sympy), not by running the paper’s scripts.

VerifiedSymbolic / matrix layer (§§3–5)

  • RmLm = φm = [[m²+1, m],[m, 1]]; collapse lemma χm(1) = −m², χm(−1) = m²+4, tr = m²+2 (m ≤ 9); Smith form of φm−I is diag(m, m), so H₁ = ℤ⊕(ℤ/m)².
  • Theorem 4.2 (period-one ⇔ det −1) sound under its stated hypotheses; the m = 6 witness A = [[1,2],[3,5]] checks completely: det −1, tr 6, λ = 3+√10 = [6̄], and A is not GL(2,ℤ)-conjugate to X₆ — the form argument is right ((1,−6,−1) ∼ x²−10y², which does not represent ±3; (3,4,−2) represents 3), corroborated by brute-force conjugator search.
  • |SL(2,ℤ/N)| = 6, 24, 48, 120 at N = 2…5 and never 24/48/120 for 6 ≤ N ≤ 12; the telescoping bound is correct. SL(2,ℤ/4) has exactly 7 involutions and no element of order 8, so ≇ 2O — verified by full enumeration.
  • φ₁ mod 5 generates a cyclic group of order 10 — the paper’s own deflation of Selection II (Scope 5.12) is accurate.
  • Fricke: tr[φm,φn] = 2−(mn(n−m))² for all m,n ≤ 6; [φ₁,φ₂] = [[11,−24],[6,−13]], trace −2; and tr[X₁,X₂] = 1, confirming the identity genuinely fails for the X’s.
  • tr φ₁ⁿ − 2 is a square exactly for odd n (Lucas identity), and φ₁³ ≁ φ₄ despite equal trace, det, and torsion.
  • Amphichirality: RmJ centralizes φm with det −1 (m ≤ 5); the RLLR counterexample matrix [[3,4],[2,3]] is correct and non-symmetric.

2 · Defects — each one verified before being asserted

Defect D-1Proposition 7.7 (“2880”) and the coupling law are not established in characteristic zero

This is the sharpest genuine gap. Prop. 7.7 asserts |⟨ρ(R),ρ(L)⟩| = 2880 for the level-2 SU(3) modular representation, “verified by enumeration at two unramified primes together with a Serre injectivity argument”, and the proof of Theorem 7.9 claims the θ-eigenspace decomposition is “verified in ℚ(ζ₆₀) arithmetic”. Neither is what is shipped: check_rho.py works entirely mod p (four primes ≡ 1 mod 60), and its own JSON note admits “the group order and the class match are enumerations at those primes”. The “Serre injectivity argument” appears nowhere in the paper or the scripts — and injectivity alone would not suffice: one must first know the characteristic-zero image is finite, which the modular relations do not give (SL(2,ℤ)/⟨⟨T¹⁵⟩⟩ is infinite; the quotients SL(2,ℤ)/⟨⟨Tⁿ⟩⟩ are finite only for n ≤ 5). Finiteness needs the congruence property of modular data (Ng–Schauenburg), which is neither cited nor proved. As certified, Prop. 7.7 and Thm. 7.9 are statements about mod-p images. Fixable: the congruence property gives the image as a quotient of SL(2,ℤ/15), and |SL(2,ℤ/15)| = 2880 — combined with the mod-p lower bound this would close the gap in three lines, but those lines must be written.

Defect D-2Internal contradiction: Scope 7.6 vs. Scope 7.16 (and the shipped suite)

Scope 7.6 (“the two ends are not symmetric”) says the non-surjection of π₁(4₁) onto 2I and A₅ is a conjecture, with “neither proof nor supporting citation”, “(It should be decidable cheaply by enumerating quotients; we have not done so.)”, and calls the count of two 2T-quotients “read off the same unshipped enumeration”. But Scope 7.16 states the same enumeration as done (“48 surjections onto 2T, and none onto A₅ or 2I, over all 3600 and 14400 pairs”), and the shipped check_quotients.py performs it. One of these passages is stale; as printed the paper simultaneously claims and disclaims the same result. Relatedly, “exactly two such quotients” is true up to Aut(2T) (2 classes) and false up to conjugacy (4 classes) — the shipped script itself prints: “The paper does not state which equivalence it means; it should.”

Defect D-3The abstract overstates Theorem 4.2

The abstract: “the hyperbolic unimodular classes whose dominant eigenvalue has purely periodic continued fraction of period one are exactly those of determinant −1”. The theorem carries the hypothesis non-negative entries; the abstract drops it, and without it the statement is false in the ⇐ direction: a det = −1 class of trace −m has dominant eigenvalue negative, with no purely periodic period-one expansion. (The ⇒ direction survives, since the eigenvalue’s minimal polynomial forces det = −1.) The abstract also asserts “their traces are exactly the integers m ≥ 1”, which silently excludes the trace −m classes the abstract’s own hypotheses admit.

Defect D-4Block (b) of the verification table is not deposited

Seven rows — including the SnapPy arithmeticity computations behind Scope 5.17’s degree table (m = 3…10), the independent word-to-field dictionary (“also verified independently rather than taken on authority” — but not shipped), the general amphichirality census, and Theorem 10.13’s stratification, which the paper itself notes is a floating-point computation. The paper flags this honestly, but for a manuscript whose central methodological claim is runnable verification, promising a table one third of which cannot be run is the single largest structural defect. Mitigation the authors should adopt: the smtfull stratification is decidable exactly over ℚ from the rational weight arrangement — I did it (Part 1) in ~100 lines; ship that instead of the floating-point version.

Defect D-5The census is still a count of statements the referee cannot read — and its labels collide with the input ledger

Census 7.21 counts 43 “links” from verify/chain_links.md. Two problems, both verified against the shipped file. (i) Label collision: the ledger’s links are numbered C1–C43 with “axioms at C3, C4, C5, C18”, while the paper’s declared inputs are (C1)–(C9) — and the same tags denote different things (ledger-C3 = “inexhaustible description” = paper-(C1); ledger-C4 = the carrier = paper-(C2); ledger-C5 = orientation = paper-(C3)). A reader who conflates the two numbering systems — as the paper’s own text invites by using the same typography — will mis-map every axiom. (ii) Content opacity: ledger rows cite objects that appear nowhere in the paper (“the weld’s θ-odd block”, “the hearing multiplication law”, “the SM record”, “the voice”, “GSWZ symmetrized product”). The earlier admitted defect (“counted a ledger a referee could not see”) is only half-repaired: the labels now travel; the statements still do not, so the six figures remain unreviewable in content. The ledger also confirms the programme’s Standard-Model ambitions (“the SM record”, “no-SM-value wall”), which the paper’s Gate-5 framing keeps offstage.

Defect D-6Undefined symbol Σ at the point of a load-bearing definition

Prop. 7.7 defines ρ by “R ↦ T and L ↦ Σ⁻¹T⁻¹Σ”. Σ is never defined; its unique occurrence in the paper is that line. (It is evidently the modular S-matrix — I verified S⁻¹T⁻¹S = L in SL(2,ℤ) — but a definition that the reader must reverse-engineer from a computation is not a definition.)

Defect D-7Figure 1 contradicts the theorem it illustrates

Theorem 7.1 warns, correctly and at length, that x = −2 (n = 1) “is the same end, reached at the other parabolic trace” and not a second cusp. Figure 1 labels that very point “hyperbolic (the other cusp)”. The label reinstates exactly the misreading Theorem 7.1’s last paragraph exists to forbid.

The proof needs: (a) BMR’s “precisely three” cyclic commensurability classes for the once-punctured torus, and (b) the implication “arithmetic ⇒ conjugate to a power of one of RL, RRLL, RRL”. 3 · The genre problem

Substantively: by the paper’s own collapse lemma, Selections I and II reduce to m² = 1 — two evaluations of one quadratic; Selection III is a one-line inequality plus a citation of Jones; Selection IV is BMR. The Lie-theoretic half is a finite exact computation plus the classical Levi classification, correctly executed and now independently confirmed. What is genuinely the paper’s own is the toral-lemma route to “rungs are Levi” (a nice observation), the 𝔢₆2O identification of the measurement plane, the rational weight-arrangement finiteness argument behind the rung spectrum, and the conjunction/framing. That is a respectable but modest mathematical core.

Around it sits an apparatus that is not mathematics: an append-only ledger of the paper’s own drafting errors, quantified self-exposure (“20 steps”, “43 links”, “four stipulations at this version”), and per-theorem accounts of what earlier drafts got wrong. Appendix C is valuable and should stay. But in the body this apparatus inverts the burden of proof — page after page anticipates the referee, concedes a weaker claim, and then argues the concession is itself a strength. A referee cannot referee a text whose primary mode is refereeing itself; the constant self-indictment functions, whatever its intent, as armor. The fix is mechanical: state each theorem, its proof, its certificate status — once — and move every “an earlier draft said” to Appendix C. My estimate is that this halves the paper at no mathematical cost.

Finally, the physics question. The paper’s Gate-5 discipline (“no physical identification is made in the body”) is formally kept. But the architecture — “charges”, “measurement”, a ℤ/6-kernel SU(5) aside, chirality/vacuum/generation “riders”, a Chern–Simons “level” action, and a ledger whose hidden rows include “the SM record” — is only intelligible as a Standard-Model derivation programme, and the disclaimers do real load-bearing work. That is not misconduct; it is a tension the referee should name: the paper wants the credit of the destination while disclaiming the journey, and the repeated “we make no physical claim” is itself a rhetorical device the abstract deploys five times.
