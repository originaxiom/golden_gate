Research note · the chirality door, computed

The Orbifold Door

Sequel to “A Road Through the ALE Fiber.” The proposal there stalled on chirality: matter from the flat E₆ fibration over Q = S³∖4₁ is vector-like, and the standard fix — points where the singularity type jumps — seemed blocked. The clever exit proposed was to quotient Q by its own isometries and let the fixed loci supply the jump structure. This note carries that out. Everything below the fold is computed, exactly, except where marked open.

Result. The door is open, and the floor plan is explicit. Q/Isom⁺ is an orbifold whose singular graph has exactly two trivalent vertices; the two involutions whose axes run to the cusp act on the 2T fiber through the outer class w ∈ 2O∖2T — the paper’s own measurement involution — making their axes E₆-folding walls, while the third involution’s axis (a closed geodesic of trace exactly 4) preserves E₆. At the two vertices the fiber group closes up to 2O, the binary group of E₇. The E₇ that the paper’s Niven theorem banished from the cone-angle family re-enters at the symmetry vertices, carried by the same w that defines the paper’s measurement plane. E₈ is provably unreachable through this door.

3 · The vertices — where the door actually opens

ComputedExactly two trivalent vertices, exhibited exactly

Solving the normalizer conditions linearly gives the isometries in closed form: n_σ = diag(1,−1) and n_τ = [[0,1],[q,0]] with q = (1+i√3)/2. Verified exactly: both are involutions, they commute, and tr(n_σn_τ) = 0 — so ⟨n_σ,n_τ⟩ ≅ ℤ₂² fixes the point p₀ = (0,1) ∈ H³ (the apex of n_τ’s axis, which lies on n_σ’s vertical axis). And tr(n_σ·γ) = 0 exactly as well, so σγ is another involution whose axis crosses the closed geodesic at the half-translation point p₁ — a second ℤ₂²-fixed point. The cusp cross-section of the quotient is the pillowcase S²(2,2,2,2), giving exactly 4 wall-ends at the cusp; two trivalent vertices supply exactly the 6 edge-germs needed (4 cusp edges + 1 connecting edge), so the count forces the graph:

At each vertex the fiber automorphisms generate ⟨2T, w-lifts⟩ = 2O: the vertices are isolated points where the E₆ locus passes through two folding walls and the local (double-cover) singularity is E₇’s. These are precisely the type-jump conical points that the chirality mechanism in G₂ compactifications requires — the structure the un-quotiented Q provably lacked. One caveat: the edge count cannot exclude additional free inner circles of Fix(τ)-type disjoint from the walls; they would carry no new vertices.

ProvedThe ceiling of this door is E₇; E₈ is unreachable

Any fiber enhancement through symmetry must extend 2T inside SU(2) by the vertex group. Isom⁺ is a 2-group, N(2T) = 2O (the paper’s Prop. 10.3, verified), ⟨2T, anything in 2O∖2T⟩ = 2O, and 2O ⊄ 2I (48 ∤ 120). Reaching 2I/E₈ would need an order-5 symmetry, and D₄ has none. So the orbifold door tops out at 2O — E₇’s binary group — and the E₆ → E₇ → (no E₈) hierarchy of this construction is a theorem about Isom(4₁), not a choice.

OpenWhat remains research

  • The actual M-theory spectrum at a (ℂ²/2T × ℝ³)/ℤ₂² twisted cone — whether the two vertices emit chiral 27s (or F₄-representation matter) — needs the local conical analysis à la Acharya–Witten; nothing here settles it.
  • The G₂ metric question from the previous note is unchanged; all statements here are equivariant-topological and exact, metric-free.
  • Amphichiral (orientation-reversing) elements of D₄ paired with antiholomorphic fiber involutions are a further, unexplored door.

Three conclusions. (i) The inner involution τ is invisible: it acts trivially on every zero mode. (ii) Four of the six vacuum moduli survive the orbifold — and the two that are frozen are exactly the degree-8 and degree-16 directions: the orbifold rigidifies precisely the measurement-plane degrees. (iii) All three 27-generation modes are σ-odd: in the inner-lift convention none survives as a 27-invariant. In the geometric (folding) picture, where σ exchanges 27 ↔ 27̄, the three modes are not destroyed but reorganized into folded F₄-type matter (one multiplet per block, in the σ-twisted combination) — still vector-like.

The pattern is now impossible to ignore: the degrees {8, 16} have appeared three independent ways — as the paper’s measurement plane 𝔢₆2O, as the nontrivial principal-sl₂ strings of the 27, and now as the σ-odd blocks of the equivariant spectrum. Indeed on the four invariant-line degrees the H¹ character of σ is exactly the negative of the paper’s Ad(w) eigenvalue pattern: (−,+,−,+) against (+,−,+,−) on (8,14,16,22). Recorded as an observation; no mechanism claimed.

VerdictChirality at zero-mode level: no — and now we know exactly where it must come from, if anywhere

The equivariant projection is sign-balanced between the 27 and 27̄ sectors (both decompose into the same blocks with the same characters), so the orbifolded field-theory spectrum remains vector-like: the chirality index at zero-mode level is zero. This closes the field-theoretic route decisively. What the computation cannot see — and what therefore becomes the sole remaining source of chirality for this construction — are intrinsically M-theoretic states localized at the two vertices themselves (twisted-sector physics of the (ℂ²/2T × ℝ³)/ℤ₂² cone, the analogue of frozen-singularity states). That is a well-posed question about one specific 7d orbifold cone, and it is genuinely open.

5 · Through the door: the vertex cone exists, and the chirality question closes to one bit

ComputedThe vertex cone is a genuine flat G₂ orbifold — supersymmetry is exact

Modeling ℝ⁷ = ℝ³ ⊕ ℍ with 2T acting by left quaternion multiplication (which fixes the hyperkähler triplet — verified, not assumed), base rotations paired with right multiplication (which rotates the triplet identically with the ℝ³ rotation), and σ carrying the extra left factor w = (1+i)/√2 ∈ 2O∖2T: the group Ĝ = ⟨2T, ĝ_τ, ĝ_σ⟩ closes with order 96, sits in SO(7), and every generator preserves the associative 3-form φ exactly. The fixed-space census over all 95 nontrivial elements (53 with 3-dimensional fixed sets — the ADE locus and edge stabilizers — and 42 with 1-dimensional, the wall axes) reproduces the singular graph of §3. Consequence: the metric obstruction (O-1 of the previous note) dissolves at the vertex — the cone (ℂ²×ℝ³)/Ĝ is an exact, flat, supersymmetric M-theory background, and “what does M-theory do at the vertex?” is now a completely specified finite question: the massless spectrum of M-theory on ℝ⁷/Ĝ for one explicit Ĝ ⊂ G₂ of order 96.

ComputedNo-go III: wall-charged matter lives in F₄, and F₄ has no complex representations

The diagram involution genuinely folds E₆: projecting the 72 roots to the involution-invariant subspace yields exactly 48 roots, 24 long and 24 short — an F₄ system — whose Weyl group, generated from the folded simples, has order 1152 and contains −1 (both verified by explicit generation). So every representation of the wall gauge symmetry is self-dual: no state charged only under the folding walls can be chiral, as a matter of Lie theory.

ComputedNo-go IV: the abelian branch is empty at unitary holonomy, and index-zero always — by the Alexander polynomial

On the abelian (Wilson-line) branch of vacua, where the unbroken gauge group is a Levi subgroup — this is where the paper’s entire rung spectrum acquires its physics meaning, since every Levi is the centralizer of a torus element — charged matter is counted by H¹(Q; ℂ_χ), which by Milnor’s theorem is nonzero exactly at roots of the Alexander polynomial. For the figure-eight, Δ(t) = t²−3t+1 has roots φ² and φ⁻² (verified exactly): the golden ratio again — real, and off the unit circle. So compact (unitary) Wilson lines meet no matter at all; charged zero modes exist only at non-unitary holonomy — the paper’s non-reality theme materialized as physics. And since Δ is palindromic (t²Δ(1/t) = Δ(t), verified — and true for every knot by duality), the roots pair as λ ↔ λ⁻¹, so h¹(χ) = h¹(χ⁻¹) and the chirality index vanishes identically on the whole branch. This no-go covers every knot complement, not just 4₁.

ClosedNo-go V: anomaly inflow — the mechanism that forces chirality everywhere else in the G₂ landscape is inert here

In every known chirality-bearing G₂ background, the chiral states at a conical point are not optional: they are required, point by point, by cancellation of the C-field’s anomalous variation (Witten, Anomaly cancellation on manifolds of G₂ holonomy, hep-th/0205184; the states themselves from Acharya–Witten, hep-th/0109152). Nonzero inflow ⟺ forced net chirality — and inflow needs a symmetry with a cubic anomaly, i.e. complex representations: U(1)s, SU(n≥3), SO(4k+2), or E₆ seeing the point. At our vertices, every ingredient of that anomaly polynomial vanishes identically: the symmetry surviving at the vertex is of F₄ type (all representations self-dual — −1 ∈ W(F₄), verified by generating all 1152 elements); the E₆ arriving along the inner edge does so as a 5d theory on an interval whose gauge multiplet content is the adjoint — real — so its folding boundary conditions produce only real F₄ content; and no U(1) survives (H⁰ = 0, verified). The required inflow at each vertex is exactly zero, and the non-chiral spectrum computed above satisfies M-theory consistency on the nose. Where Acharya–Witten’s cones are compelled to emit chiral fermions, this cone is compelled to emit none.

ClosedNo-go VI: all eight discrete-torsion variants inherit the same obstruction

The torsion group is computed, not recalled: H³(ℤ₂², U(1)) ≅ H₃(ℤ₂², ℤ) = ℤ₂³ by exact Smith normal form on the bar resolution — so the construction admits 8 C-field variants, not the 2 an earlier phrasing of this note guessed. By the frozen-singularity results (de Boer–Dijkgraaf et al., Triples, fluxes, and strings, §4.6.2; Tachikawa, arXiv:1508.06679 and Frozen singularities in M and F theory), fractional C-field flux on an ADE locus freezes it: the gauge symmetry is reduced toward non-simply-laced algebras — Sp, SO(odd), F₄, G₂ — every one of which has only self-dual representations. Freezing removes complex representations; it never creates them. All eight variants therefore land inside the same reality obstruction as no-gos III and V.

VerdictThe conclusion, and its epistemic register

M-theory on the D₄-orbifold of the ALE-fibered figure-eight geometry produces no chiral matter, in any of its eight discrete-torsion variants, through any known mechanism. Six independent closures, each for a structural reason: 27/27̄ pairing at the geometric vacuum; sign-balance of the equivariant projection; F₄ reality on the walls; Alexander palindromy on the abelian branch (valid for every knot); vanishing anomaly inflow at the vertices; and reality of all frozen variants. The first four are mathematics; the last two are physics at the standard rigor of the G₂ literature (inflow consistency and the frozen-singularity classification taken as established). What would overturn the verdict is not a better computation but a new M-theory mechanism — one that manufactures complex representations where reality theorems forbid them. Until such a mechanism exists, the honest summary is: the figure-eight knot does not make chiral matter. The paper under review prized exactly this kind of result — a negative established structurally rather than by exhaustion — and it is fitting that its physics shadow ends in one.

6 · The hole, tested: non-geometric vacua

ComputedAll irreducible SL(3) vacua with unipotent boundary, found and tested

The no-gos above quantified over the vacua analyzed, not all vacua — the honest hole was the non-geometric components of the flat-connection variety. Three structural facts, uniform across all components. (i) The chirality index vanishes at every vacuum — and not vacuously: the non-geometric vacua carry genuine charged matter (h¹ = 1 in both the 3 and the 3̄), an honest vector-like pair each. (ii) Some element of the knot’s own symmetry group ℤ₂² realizes dualization (ρ∘φ ≅ ρ*) on every component — the earlier guess that inversion always does it was refuted (inversion fixes the Falbel vacuum); the corrected statement is that the three involutions distribute the roles fix/dualize/dualize differently on each component, realizing all three possible patterns. (iii) ρ* ≅ ρ̄ (Galois conjugate) on every component: each vacuum is conjugate-self-dual. Since complex conjugation on the character variety is what the knot’s amphichirality implements geometrically, the mechanism killing chirality is the object’s oldest property in this story: the amphichiral knot yields achiral matter — duality of representations is realized by the mirror symmetry of the knot itself.

Scope that remains open, stated exactly: components with non-unipotent boundary holonomy (deformations away from the parabolic locus), representations with non-regular unipotent meridians (a second slice, unsearched), and E₆ flat connections factoring through nothing smaller — plus every construction outside this compactification family. Within everything searched: six no-gos, four component families, one mechanism, zero chirality.
